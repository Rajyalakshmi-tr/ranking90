Ranking Methodology

Our comprehensive ranking methodology evaluates universities across multiple key indicators to provide a fair and accurate assessment of institutional performance.

30%

Academic Reputation

Based on global survey responses from academics who are asked to identify the best institutions in their field of expertise.

20%

Employer Reputation

Derived from global surveys of employers who identify the institutions that produce the best graduates.

15%

Faculty Student Ratio

Measures the teaching environment by assessing the ratio of faculty members to students.

20%

Citations per Faculty

Evaluates research impact by measuring the number of citations received by faculty publications.

5%

International Faculty

Assesses the international diversity of the faculty body.

5%

International Students

Measures the international diversity of the student body.

5%

International Research Network

Evaluates the extent of international research collaboration across institutions worldwide.

Overview: TR World University Ranking uses a weighted scoring system that combines quantitative data and qualitative assessments from academic experts, employers, and institutional representatives. Our methodology is designed to be transparent, reproducible, and globally applicable.

Data Sources: Scopus bibliometric database for research metrics, global academic surveys for reputation indicators, institutional data submissions for faculty and student ratios, international collaboration data from research publications, and employer surveys for graduate employability assessment.

Scoring Process: Data collection from verified sources and institutional submissions → Data validation and normalization for fair comparison → Application of weightings to each indicator → Calculation of overall scores and rankings → Expert review and quality assurance → Final ranking publication.

Methodology Updates: Our methodology is reviewed annually to ensure it remains relevant and reflects the evolving landscape of higher education. Changes are implemented transparently with detailed explanations provided in our annual methodology reports.
